About Casa di Moni

Italian flavour, warm hospitality, and a sweet Ottawa experience.

Located in the heart of Ottawa’s ByWard Market, Casa di Moni offers authentic Italian dishes, artisan gelato, barista coffee, pastries, frozen treats and more.

Whether you are looking for a quick coffee, a family dessert, or a refreshing gelato, Casa di Moni has something for everyone. To satisfy your cravings in a hurry, the café is also available through Uber Eats, DoorDash and SkipTheDishes.
